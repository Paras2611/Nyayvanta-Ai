# Nyayavanta: Claim Verification Pipeline

> **Core Mandate**: *"Never make AI look magical. Make its evidence visible."*  
> **Rule**: Never simulate verification. Never rely on color alone. If a source cannot be established, explicitly declare it unverified.

---

## 1. Claim Verification Architecture

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    GeneratedAnswer["Draft Legal Reasoning Generated by Model"] --> Decompose["Claim Extraction & Decomposition\n(Isolate factual, procedural, and statutory assertions)"]
    
    Decompose --> ClaimList["List of Atomic Claims\n{claimStatement, category, importance}"]
    RetrievedPassages["Top-K Retrieved Legal Evidence Passages"] --> GroundingCheck["Passage Grounding & NLI Alignment Engine"]
    ClaimList --> GroundingCheck

    GroundingCheck --> OverlapEval["Lexical & Semantic Overlap Calculation\n(Check verbatim section match & authority attribution)"]

    OverlapEval --> DecisionTree{"Match Threshold"}
    DecisionTree -->|">= 0.35 with Authoritative Statute|" VerdictSupported["Status: SUPPORTED\nUI Badge: VERIFIED\nConfidence: 88-97%"]
    DecisionTree -->|"0.20 - 0.35 with Principle|" VerdictPartial["Status: SUPPORTED\nUI Badge: PARTIALLY_VERIFIED\nConfidence: 72-85%"]
    DecisionTree -->|"< 0.20 or Missing Source|" VerdictUnverified["Status: NOT_ENOUGH_EVIDENCE\nUI Badge: UNVERIFIED\nConfidence: 45%\n'Nyayavanta could not verify this claim'"]
    DecisionTree -->|"Contradicts Statutory Text|" VerdictConflict["Status: CONTRADICTED\nUI Badge: CONFLICTING\nConfidence: High Conflict"]

    VerdictSupported --> CitationMapping["Citation & Provenance Mapping\n(Attach exact Act, Section, Citation, URL)"]
    VerdictPartial --> CitationMapping
    VerdictUnverified --> CitationMapping
    VerdictConflict --> CitationMapping

    CitationMapping --> EvidencePanel["Evidence & Sources Panel Display"]
```

## 2. Status & Badge Standards

| Internal Status | UI Badge | Visual Icon | Criteria |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `SUPPORTED` | `VERIFIED` | ShieldCheck (Green) | Assertion is directly grounded in primary Bare Act text or binding Supreme Court ruling. |
| `SUPPORTED` | `PARTIALLY_VERIFIED` | AlertCircle (Amber) | Substantively aligned with legal doctrine, but specific contract terms or dates require primary document review. |
| `NOT_ENOUGH_EVIDENCE` | `UNVERIFIED` | HelpCircle (Slate) | Available legal corpus does not establish the claim. Explicit text: *"Nyayavanta could not verify this claim against available legal sources."* |
| `CONTRADICTED` | `CONFLICTING` | XCircle (Red) | Claim contradicts explicit statutory prohibitions (e.g. alleging non-compete is enforceable in India). |

### Confidence Score Calculation
The system calculates confidence as a factor of verified claims:
$$\text{Confidence Score} = \min\left(80.0 + (\text{Verified Claims Count} \times 4.0), 96.0\right)$$
Every confidence card explicitly carries the statutory disclaimer:
> *"System confidence is an indicator of statutory source grounding and is NOT a legal correctness guarantee or outcome warranty."*
